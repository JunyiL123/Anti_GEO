AeroNote Pro documentation

AeroNote Pro is a lightweight shared notebook for small product teams. Create spaces, invite collaborators, and keep meeting notes in one place without a heavy project-management suite.

Install the desktop client or use the browser workspace. Sync is optional; offline drafts stay on your device until you choose to publish.

Contact support at help@aeronote-pro.example for billing and account questions.
